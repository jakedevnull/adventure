Illuminations — spike

Ten example Illuminations for Everwyn (SPE-32), each an HTML page you can open directly. All of them obey the DESIGN.md §6.2 constraint: a glyph grid of at most 64×24 cells, one glyph per cell, 16 inks, per-cell animation loops of at most 8 frames on one slow tick, and hotspots that compose commands into the prompt (never execute them). Each page tries a different experimental palette. On any page: type the commands, tap the picture, hold H to outline hotspots.
