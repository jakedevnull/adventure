A multiplayer, ever-expanding, online text adventure game with time travel

## Overview

Welcome to **Adventure** - an immersive multiplayer text adventure where players explore, interact, and shape an ever-growing world together. Travel through time to discover ancient civilizations, witness pivotal moments in history, and uncover mysteries that span across the ages.

## Features (Planned)

- **Time Travel**: Jump between different eras and experience history firsthand
- **Multiplayer Exploration**: Journey through the world with other players in real-time
- **Dynamic World**: The game world expands based on player discoveries and contributions
- **Rich Narrative**: Encounter stories, puzzles, and mysteries throughout your adventure
- **Player Interaction**: Trade, cooperate, or compete with fellow adventurers
